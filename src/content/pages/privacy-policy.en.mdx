## Introduction

BeatAPI is an AI visual generation and design workspace for ecommerce teams. This Privacy Policy explains how BeatAPI collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit the website, create an account, upload assets, generate images or videos, purchase a plan, or contact support.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following information to provide and improve BeatAPI:

1. **Account information** — name, email address, avatar, login method, account status, and security records.
2. **Uploads and generated content** — product images, reference images, brand assets, prompts, parameters, generated outputs, project records, and history versions.
3. **Usage data** — page visits, feature interactions, generation counts, credit usage, template usage, error logs, and performance data.
4. **Device and log data** — IP address, browser, device type, operating system, language, access time, and basic security logs.
5. **Payment and subscription data** — order number, plan, amount, payment status, and invoice-related information. Full card numbers are handled by third-party payment processors and are not stored on our servers.
6. **Support information** — tickets, emails, attachments, and communication history.
7. **Cookies and local storage** — used for sessions, preferences, security, analytics, and product experience.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide account, login, project, template, history, generation, and credit features.
- Process orders, subscriptions, refunds, invoices, and payment security checks.
- Operate AI models, storage, email, analytics, and anti-abuse services.
- Diagnose bugs, improve generation quality, optimize performance, and improve the product.
- Detect fraud, abuse, attacks, spam, and violations of our terms.
- Send service notices, security alerts, billing updates, product updates, and support replies.
- Comply with law, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect BeatAPI, users, and third parties.

## AI Generation and Third-Party Services

BeatAPI may send your inputs, parameters, and necessary task data to third-party AI model providers, cloud storage, payment processors, email providers, analytics services, or security providers to complete generation, storage, payment, notification, and protection workflows. We share only what is needed to provide the service and require providers to handle data under contract, platform rules, and applicable law.

Do not upload product images, portraits, logos, copyrighted works, confidential information, or other protected materials unless you have the rights to use them. You are responsible for ensuring that your inputs and published outputs are properly licensed.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these cases:

- With vendors that help operate BeatAPI, such as hosting, databases, object storage, AI models, payments, email, support, analytics, and security services.
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, asset transfer, or similar transaction.
- To comply with law, regulation, court orders, or lawful government requests.
- To investigate, prevent, or address fraud, abuse, security incidents, infringement claims, or violations of our terms.
- With your consent.

## Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, fulfill contracts, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal requirements, and protect security. When you delete projects, assets, or your account, related data may be removed from active systems, but backups, audit logs, payment records, and compliance records may be retained for a reasonable period.

## Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, key management, logging, and permission isolation to protect information. No internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your password, login methods, and team permissions.

## Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal information, close your account, or unsubscribe from non-essential notifications. Some information may be retained for billing, risk control, security, legal, or dispute-resolution purposes.

## Children

BeatAPI is not intended for children. If you believe a minor has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to address it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes may be communicated by updating the date on this page, in-product notice, or another reasonable method. Continued use of BeatAPI means you accept the updated policy.
